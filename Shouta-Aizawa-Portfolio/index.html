About Me

An Educator Focused on Student Potential

I am Shouta Aizawa, a secondary school teacher at U.A. High School. My approach to education is built around recognising the individual strengths of students and encouraging them to develop their abilities.

I believe that education should extend beyond delivering content. Students should be challenged to think independently, develop confidence, and understand how to apply their knowledge in practical situations.

My teaching philosophy places importance on discipline, adaptability, resilience, and individual growth. I aim to provide students with opportunities to discover their potential while supporting them through their challenges.

"The purpose of education is to help students recognise what they are capable of achieving."
